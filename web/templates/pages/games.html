Let's play together

Videogames are part of my lifestyle. I like a lot of different genres: fast-paced first person shooters, survival, simulation and adventure are just some of the types of games that I enjoy playing.

I am a fierce defence of DRM-free software, and this includes games. I refuse to buy games that contain anti-consumer spyware named as Denuvo, which breaks compatibility with GNU/Linux. (EasyAntiCheat, you too)

I play in a Windows 10 machine, although my plan is to move to Debian Bullseye as soon as possible given that now I've got plenty of horsepower to tinker with it.

My most played games right now include:

Tom Clancy's Rainbow Six Siege
Dead by Daylight
Call of Duty: Modern Warfare

And many more! I also love Minecraft and the Animal Crossing series.
