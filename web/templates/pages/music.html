Music

🎵🎵🎵

Music is something I couldn't live without. I mostly listen to pop and EDM music, although I am curious about other genres except reggaeton. I can't stand it.

I wouldn't call myself an audiophile, but I like to listen to music without annoyances and in good quality. That's one of the reasons of why I still use the "old and rusty" iPods. No cell data, no bluetooth, zero notifications.

Why iPods among other MP3 players then? Mainly because of availability and community support. They can hold A LOT of storage and they are widely available, whether its spare parts or a "new" unit itself.

All my iPods are flashed with Rockbox and include FLACs, if possible. I don't use Spotify nor any other music streaming service.

My music library holds more than 2500 different songs and +70 GiB of music. My earphones are the Sennheiser CX 5.00G. I'll get some proper headphones at some point.
